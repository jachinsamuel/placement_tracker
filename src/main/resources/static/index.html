Candidate Ranking Algorithm Formula

The portal uses a 100% rule-based, deterministic scoring algorithm. Each component is normalized out of 100 before applying user-configurable weights:

Score = (Normalized_CGPA × w₁%) + (Skill_Match_% × w₂%) + (Cert_Score × w₃%)
  • Normalized CGPA: (CGPA / 10.0) × 100.0
  • Skill Match %: (Matched_Required_Skills / Total_Required_Skills) × 100.0
  • Certifications Score: Maintained score out of 100 (Default: 0.0)
  • Tie-Breaking Rule: If two candidates have identical composite scores, higher CGPA takes precedence.
